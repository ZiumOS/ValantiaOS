01THE IDEA

Less setup.
More possibility.

ValantiaOS brings together a Debian base, an approachable XFCE desktop, and a handful of gaming essentials in a bootable live environment.

Try it without installing. Look around. See what works on your hardware. This is an experimental project, and every release is a step in the process—not a promise that every machine is supported.
